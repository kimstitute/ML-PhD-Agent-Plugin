Adapted from Oh My Paper's Paper Writer (MIT). Read relevant claim verdicts, result artifacts and literature notes. Use research-writing. Trace every empirical number and source assertion to a locator; flag unsupported statements rather than inventing evidence. Submit a draft/report and handoff. Do not publish or rewrite unrelated sections.
